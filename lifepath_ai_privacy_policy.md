# Privacy Policy for LifePath AI

**Last updated:** September 20, 2026

LifePath AI ("we", "our", or "the app") provides an AI-powered life story, guided journaling, and personal memoir reflection platform designed to help users document their life journey, capture meaningful milestones, and preserve personal legacy. Your personal privacy and the intimacy of your memories are our highest priority.

---

## 1. Information We Collect and Process

### a. User-Generated Memoirs and Entries
- **Personal Reflections & Journal Entries:** Memories, life experiences, letters to your future self, and thoughts you write or dictate within LifePath AI are stored locally on your device storage by default.
- **Pillar Categorization & Art Styles:** Pillar classifications (Health, Wealth, Wisdom, Connection) and aesthetic selections are processed to generate contextual reflections and visual themes.
- **Local Storage:** Your entries remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share them.

### b. AI Reflection & Prompt Processing
- When you use AI-assisted prompt generation or reflection features, your short memory prompt is processed ephemerally solely to synthesize thoughtful reflection text and butterfly effect insights.
- We do not use your private personal journal entries or memoirs to train public foundational AI models.

### c. Anonymous Technical Data
- If enabled via Google Play Services, we may receive non-identifiable diagnostic data (such as app crash logs, device model, operating system version, and general performance metrics) to identify bugs and ensure stability across Android devices.

---

## 2. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

LifePath AI offers optional premium tiers (e.g., Legacy Unlimited, Lifetime Patron). All financial transactions and payment card processing are handled securely and directly through the **Google Play Store Billing API**. We do not collect, process, or store credit card details or bank account information.

---

## 3. Data Retention and User Control

- **Complete Data Ownership:** You retain full ownership of all memoirs, journal entries, and personal milestones created within the app.
- **Exporting and Deleting:** You can clear your timeline or reset saved journal entries at any time via the in-app settings or by clearing the application data in your Android device settings.
- **No Third-Party Brokers:** We never sell, rent, or trade your personal journal entries or memoirs to third-party data brokers or advertising networks.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

LifePath AI is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be published directly within the app or on this hosted policy page with an updated revision date.
